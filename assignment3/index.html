Who we are

Yamaat is a remote place in the far west of Mongolia where Kazakh nomadic families have lived for centuries. Clear rivers, dense forests and wild animals surround the summer camps.

Our family-run agency welcomes small groups who want to experience this landscape respectfully: no mobile signal, no electricity, only the steppe and the stars.
